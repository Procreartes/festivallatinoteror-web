30 years of Latin flavour

The Festival Latino Villa de Teror is one of the highlights of summer in Gran Canaria and part of the town's cultural identity. Live music, dancing and a family atmosphere out in the street, with no tickets needed.

  • 30editions held in Teror
  • 5,000+people at the 30th edition
  • 6festival days, with dance workshops all week
  • €0Every activity is free

The 30th edition

A whole week of music and dance that built up day by day to the big international night on Saturday 25 July 2026.

Monday 20 – Thursday 23

Dance workshops

Free salsa, bachata and Latin dance classes every evening on the Alameda de Pío XII, 8:30–10:30 pm, taught by Ancor and Ayose Domínguez of the Paso a Paso Las Palmas academy.

  • Salsa
  • Bachata
  • Alameda de Pío XII

Friday 24 · from 11 am

Gran Canaria Joven edition

A day for young audiences with workshops, the «History of Salsa» exhibition, screenings, street parades and stands from local associations. Supported by the Education and Youth Department of the Cabildo de Gran Canaria.

  • Dos de Azúcar
  • Havana 500
  • Yet Garbey
  • Idel El Mulatito
  • DJ Ruymán Melián

Saturday 25 · from 7 pm

International gala

Gran Canaria's dance academies and La Golosina warmed up the square for Tony & Mimy Succar, who came from Miami with Kenyi Succar, and Tromboranga, Europe's benchmark for hard salsa. DJ Yotu closed the night.

  • Tony & Mimy Succar
  • Tromboranga
  • La Golosina
  • Paso a Paso Las Palmas
  • DJ Yotu

A festival in the street

Together for a greener festival

The Festival Latino de Teror Sustainability Plan aims to reduce the environmental impact of the event while supporting social inclusion and the local economy.

  • Sustainable mobility

    Encouraging public transport and sustainable ways of getting to Teror.

  • Water & energy

    Saving water and energy during set-up and on festival days.

  • Waste

    Less waste, reused infrastructure and a circular economy.

  • Inclusion

    Accessibility, fair working conditions, support for local shops and public awareness.

Plenty to see: Teror

Teror combines history, traditional architecture and nature just minutes from Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. The Basilica of Our Lady of the Pine, its streets of Canarian balconies, its mountains and its market make it the perfect place to spend a few relaxed hours or days.

Make the most of the festival and stay to discover it.
