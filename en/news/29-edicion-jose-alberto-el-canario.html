3 July 2025 · Teror Press Office

The 29th edition features José Alberto «El Canario», Estrellas de Buena Vista y Más, Pinto Picasso and La Golosina

The 29th Festival Latino Villa de Teror arrives on 25 July with a stellar line-up led by Dominican singer José Alberto «El Canario», a true icon of Latin music. Also on the bill are Puerto Rico's Pinto Picasso, Cuba's Estrellas de Buena Vista y Más and Gran Canaria's own La Golosina, for a festive night of Latin rhythm and flavour.

Organised by the Festivities Department of the Teror Town Council, the Festival Latino Villa de Teror is once again one of the most eagerly awaited summer events in Gran Canaria, offering a unique experience for all audiences, with free admission and a festive, family atmosphere.

Free entertainment and concerts

Plaza de Sintes will host this long-running festival once again, starting on 25 July at 7 pm with entertainment from the Paso a Paso Las Palmas academy and Gran Canaria's dance academies, followed by a concert by La Golosina from 7:30 pm.

At 9 pm, the stage will fill with Cuban son as Estrellas de Buena Vista y Más, heirs to the legendary Buena Vista Social Club, take over.

At 11 pm comes the urban, colourful bachata of Pinto Picasso, one of the most exciting emerging voices in Latin music, from Puerto Rico.

At midnight, one of salsa's great names, Dominican José Alberto «El Canario», will close the festival with all his charisma and vocal power.

The night will end with an SBK session by DJ Nau, playing the best of salsa, bachata and kizomba.

Free Latin dance classes, 21–24 July

Ahead of the festival, Teror warms up with four days of Latin dance classes on the Alameda de Pío XII, from 21 to 24 July, 8–10 pm, taught free of charge by Ancor and Ayose Domínguez of the Paso a Paso Las Palmas academy, plus an SBK session.

José Alberto «El Canario»

With more than four decades on stage, José Alberto «El Canario» is a legendary salsa voice and a symbol of elegance and evolution in the genre. Born in Santo Domingo (Dominican Republic) in 1958, he moved as a young man to Puerto Rico and then to New York, where he developed as an artist during salsa's golden age. His nickname comes from a unique skill: improvising whistled melodies as fluently as others play the flute, which became his personal trademark.

In the 1970s he became lead singer of the legendary Típica 73, one of the first orchestras to fuse salsa with jazz and timba. His big breakthrough came in the 1980s, when he founded his own band and embraced a more romantic style without losing the essence of Caribbean son. With hits such as Sueño contigo, Discúlpeme señora and Bailemos otra vez, he was one of the driving forces of salsa romántica.

He has taken his music across Latin America, Europe, Asia and Africa, and shared the stage with legends such as Celia Cruz, Tito Puente, Rubén Blades, Oscar D'León and Johnny Pacheco. His career has been recognised with gold and platinum records and with Latin Grammys in 2013, 2015 and 2018.

Estrellas de Buena Vista y Más

Estrellas de Buena Vista y Más was founded in 2021 under the musical direction of Pancho Amat, recognised as the world's finest tres player, with the mission of preserving the legacy of the Buena Vista Social Club. Bringing together original members and new generations of musicians, the group conveys the deepest roots of Cuban music while embracing contemporary influences, reviving its golden age and projecting it into the future on stages around the world.

Pinto Picasso

A Puerto Rican singer, songwriter and producer, Pinto Picasso has worked with urban music artists including Plan B, Tito «El Bambino», Ñengo Flow, J Alvarez, Tempo, Franco El Gorila, Trebol Clan and Daddy Yankee. He represented Puerto Rico on NBC's a cappella show «The Sing-Off» and in 2015 opened Daddy Yankee's European tour. He has headlined four tours in Europe and two in Latin America, including the All Music Fest in Lima in front of 45,000 people.

In recent years he has focused on bachata, fusing it with R&B and urban rhythms. His latest album, «CASAGEMAS», came out in 2025.

La Golosina

La Golosina is the salsa orchestra with a Canarian stamp that brings local flavour to the festival. With more than two decades on stage, this Gran Canaria band has won audiences over with rhythm, professionalism and a repertoire that combines salsa classics with original songs. It will open the night of 25 July.
