Teror gets ready for the 2025 Festival Latino: rhythm, dance and flavour in Plaza de Sintes

The Teror Town Council, through its Festivities Department, announces a new edition of the iconic Festival Latino Villa de Teror, to be held on Friday 25 July in Plaza de Sintes.

Starting at 7 pm, the event will once again fill the heart of the town with the hottest rhythms of the Caribbean and Latin America, a summer must in Gran Canaria.

As a prelude to the big show, free salsa, bachata and kizomba classes will be held from 21 to 24 July on the Alameda de Pío XII, from 8 to 10 pm, so that nobody misses out on dancing to the festival's beat.

With free admission and a festive atmosphere, the Festival Latino Villa de Teror promises an unforgettable night of dancing and Latin culture in one of the island's most iconic spots.
