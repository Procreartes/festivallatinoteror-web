Tony & Mimy Succar headline the 30th Festival Latino Villa de Teror

On Saturday 25 July, from 7 pm, Plaza de Sintes will host the main event of the thirtieth Festival Latino Villa de Teror, a firm fixture in the cultural calendar of the Canary Islands that will once again fill the town with music, dance and Latin rhythms.

The show will be headlined by Tony & Mimy Succar, joined by Kenyi Succar. Winners of two Grammy awards, Tony and Mimy will bring Teror a performance full of energy, emotion and Latin fusion, sharing their talent and family chemistry on stage.

The line-up is completed by Tromboranga, one of today's most acclaimed salsa bands, known for its powerful sound, its leading trombones and a show made for dancing.

The day will start at 7 pm with activities for all ages and performances by Paso a Paso Las Palmas, La Golosina, Gran Canaria's dance academies and DJ sets, turning Plaza de Sintes into a great meeting point for lovers of Latin music and culture.

The 30th Festival Latino Villa de Teror is organised by the Teror Town Council in collaboration with the Government of the Canary Islands, Islas Canarias Latitud de Vida and Turismo de las Islas Canarias.

Admission is free until full capacity is reached.

Event details

  • Date: Saturday 25 July 2026.
  • Time: from 7 pm.
  • Venue: Plaza de Sintes, Teror.
  • Headliners: Tony & Mimy Succar, with Kenyi Succar, and Tromboranga.
  • Free admission.
