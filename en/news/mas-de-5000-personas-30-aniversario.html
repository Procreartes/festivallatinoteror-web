27 July 2026 · Teror Press Office

More than 5,000 people celebrated the 30th anniversary of the Festival Latino in Teror

The 30th Festival Latino Villa de Teror brought more than 5,000 people to Plaza de Sintes, in the most ambitious edition of its three-decade history.

Organised by the Teror Town Council, the festival grew throughout a whole week, with Latin dance workshops every evening, and peaked on Saturday 25 July with an international gala headlined by Peru's Tony & Mimy Succar, the first artists from their country to win a Grammy.

Thirty years after it began, the festival once again proved to be one of the highlights of summer in Gran Canaria and part of the cultural identity of the town. The anniversary edition brought together local talent and top international names.

All week: salsa workshops on the Alameda

The festival began on Monday 20 July with its traditional Latin dance classes, which filled the Alameda de Pío XII every evening. Brothers Ancor and Ayose Domínguez, of the Paso a Paso Las Palmas academy, taught salsa, bachata and other Latin rhythms free of charge until Thursday 23, from 8:30 to 10:30 pm, as a warm-up for the big weekend.

Friday 24: the «Gran Canaria Joven» edition

From 11 am, Plaza de Sintes became a large space for music, culture and dance, with participatory workshops, exhibitions such as the History of Salsa, screenings, street parades and concerts by Dos de Azúcar, Havana 500, Yet Garbey, Idel El Mulatito and DJ Ruymán Melián. This day, aimed especially at young audiences, was supported by the Education and Youth Department of the Cabildo de Gran Canaria.

Saturday 25: the big day

From 7 pm, the dance academies of Gran Canaria, with Paso a Paso Las Palmas, and the orchestra La Golosina warmed up the square before the big concerts: Tony & Mimy Succar, who came from Miami with Kenyi Succar, and Tromboranga, Europe's benchmark for hard salsa. DJ Yotu closed the night. The international gala was sponsored by the Government of the Canary Islands through the «Islas Canarias» brand (Promotur Turismo Canarias, S.A.).

All festival activities, from the dance classes to the concerts, were free.
