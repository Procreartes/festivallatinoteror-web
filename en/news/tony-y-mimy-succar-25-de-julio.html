Tony & Mimy Succar will play the 30th Festival Latino Villa de Teror on 25 July

Teror will celebrate the most special edition of its Festival Latino with a top international line-up led by Tony & Mimy Succar, alongside Kenyi Succar and Jean Rodríguez, Tromboranga and La Golosina.

The Festival Latino Villa de Teror 2026 celebrates its 30th edition on 25 July, establishing itself as one of the most important music events of the summer in the Canary Islands, bringing together leading names in Latin music in a unique setting.

The festival will feature a star performance by Tony & Mimy Succar, together with Kenyi Succar and Jean Rodríguez, who bring from Miami a show that blends tradition and modernity with spectacular staging. From Gran Canaria, audiences will also enjoy the salsa power of La Golosina, and from Barcelona comes the salsa brava of Tromboranga.

On Saturday 25 July, Plaza de Sintes will host a night full of rhythm, flavour and energy with free admission, inviting everyone to enjoy an event that is already part of the town's cultural identity.

This special edition, marking three decades, reinforces the Teror Town Council's commitment to Latin music as a high-quality cultural and festive meeting point for all audiences, bringing together artists of national and international renown.

The Festival Latino Villa de Teror is once again a meeting point for lovers of salsa and Latin rhythms, consolidating its festive character and its place in the cultural calendar of the Canary Islands.
